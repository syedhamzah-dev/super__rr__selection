Methodology, Formulas & Governance Register

Transparent documentation of mathematical scoring engines, shrinkage calibration, and governance constraints.

Performance Rating (PR, 0–100)

Combines pressure-adjusted baseline scoring/economy with clutch performance multipliers and Empirical Bayes credibility shrinkage toward tournament prior mean.

PR = Z * Raw_Score + (1 - Z) * Prior_Mean

Where Z is the volume credibility weight bounded strictly in [0.0, 1.0].

Evidence Reliability Score (ERS, 0–100)

Multi-dimensional exposure metric capturing overall volume, clutch sample size, matches played, and tournament breadth through linear saturation functions.

ERS = 0.40 * S_clutch + 0.25 * S_overall + 0.20 * S_matches + 0.15 * S_comps

Calibrated against linear targets: 120 clutch deliveries, 300 total deliveries, 25 matches, 3 competitions.

Tiers: Strong Evidence (ERS ≥ 60, Clutch ≥ 40, Matches ≥ 10) | Moderate Evidence (ERS ≥ 35, Clutch ≥ 20, Matches ≥ 5).

Statistical Stability Score (SSS, 0–100; Top-Tier Impact Retention Index)

Top-Tier Impact Retention Index evaluating candidate resilience across 1,000 bootstrap resamples, rank volatility (Top-20 retention frequency), volume concentration (HHI), and leave-one-match-out (LOMO) shifts.

SSS = 0.35 * Boot_Rel + 0.30 * Top20_Freq + 0.20 * (1 - HHI) + 0.15 * LOMO_Res

Specifically calibrated to identify elite performers capable of sustained campaign impact without single-match sample dependency.

Recruitment Decision Score (RDH, 0–90 Scale)

Synthesizes performance, reliability, stability, and tactical fit while penalizing acquisition risk. Operates on a strict 0.00 to 90.00 theoretical scale.

RDH = clip(0.30*PR + 0.20*ERS + 0.20*SSS + 0.20*TFS - 0.10*ARS, 0.0, 90.0)

* Theoretical maximum is 90.00 because positive weights sum to 0.90. Never displayed as a percentage or win probability.
