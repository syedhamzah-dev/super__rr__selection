💡 Key Analytical Findings & Project Insights
Traceable Outputs
1. Non-Linear Value of Clutch Deliveries

Analysis of 200,999 deliveries reveals a universal 14.8% decline in batting strike rate under extreme pressure (PI ≥ 6.0). Elite outliers like Tim David (179.3 clutch SR) and Naman Dhir (168.2 clutch SR) provide disproportionate win probability impact in death-overs chases.

2. Empirical Bayes Reliability Decoupling

By decoupling Performance Rating (PR) from Evidence Reliability (ERS), we eliminate small-sample bias. Batters requiring ≥40 clutch balls and bowlers requiring ≥40 clutch deliveries achieved Strong Evidence tier status (201 batters, 206 bowlers).

3. Bootstrap Ranking Stability (SSS)

1,000 cluster-preserving match bootstrap iterations show that elite death bowlers (e.g., Benjamin Sears, Liam Dawson) exhibit top-tier retention indices > 70.0, proving their historical effectiveness is not an artifact of single outlier performances.

4. 100 BAU Auction Capital Conservation

Across 5 strategic allocation scenarios, allocating 35–45 BAU to death bowling and 30–45 BAU to lower-order finishing maximizes squad expected surplus value while preserving budget flexibility against speculative bidding inflation.

📖 Methodology, Metric Glossary & Analytical Limitations

The SupeRR Intelligence platform synthesizes 883 audited matches (2022–2026) across six mathematically defined evaluation dimensions:

PR — Performance Rating (0–100)

Evaluates clutch execution using Bayesian shrinkage across strike rate, boundary %, dot %, and phase-adjusted economy. Higher is better.

ERS — Evidence Reliability Score (0–90)

Quantifies sample volume: clutch balls, total deliveries, matches, and multi-tournament exposure. Higher represents greater statistical confidence.

SSS — Statistical Stability Score (0–100)

Top-Tier Impact Retention Index from 1,000 bootstrap iterations measuring ranking volatility and match-removal resilience.

TFS — Tactical Fit Score (0–100)

Measures candidate capability alignment with Rajasthan Royals' primary tactical voids: death-overs bowling and lower-order finishing.

ARS — Acquisition Risk Score (0–100)

Quantifies bidding war inflation, overseas slot competition, and player unavailability. Lower is preferable.

RDH — Recruitment Decision Heuristic (0–90)

Composite decision formula: 0.30*PR + 0.20*ERS + 0.20*SSS + 0.20*TFS - 0.10*ARS. Theoretical range strictly [0.0, 90.0].

⚠️ Important Analytical Limitations: Historical multi-tournament pressure performance provides empirical evaluation of past execution under stress. It is not an unconditional guarantee of future IPL performance. All actionable candidates are potential targets whose auction availability depends on official player registration.

Methodology, Formulas & Governance Register

Transparent documentation of mathematical scoring engines, shrinkage calibration, and governance constraints.

Performance Rating (PR, 0–100)

Combines pressure-adjusted baseline scoring/economy with clutch performance multipliers and Empirical Bayes credibility shrinkage toward tournament prior mean.

PR = Z * Raw_Score + (1 - Z) * Prior_Mean

Where Z is the volume credibility weight bounded strictly in [0.0, 1.0].

Evidence Reliability Score (ERS, 0–100)

Multi-dimensional exposure metric capturing overall volume, clutch sample size, matches played, and tournament breadth through linear saturation functions.

ERS = 0.40 * S_clutch + 0.25 * S_overall + 0.20 * S_matches + 0.15 * S_comps

Calibrated against linear targets: 120 clutch deliveries, 300 total deliveries, 25 matches, 3 competitions.

Tiers: Strong Evidence (ERS ≥ 60, Clutch ≥ 40, Matches ≥ 10) | Moderate Evidence (ERS ≥ 35, Clutch ≥ 20, Matches ≥ 5).

Statistical Stability Score (SSS, 0–100; Top-Tier Impact Retention Index)

Top-Tier Impact Retention Index evaluating candidate resilience across 1,000 bootstrap resamples, rank volatility (Top-20 retention frequency), volume concentration (HHI), and leave-one-match-out (LOMO) shifts.

SSS = 0.35 * Boot_Rel + 0.30 * Top20_Freq + 0.20 * (1 - HHI) + 0.15 * LOMO_Res

Specifically calibrated to identify elite performers capable of sustained campaign impact without single-match sample dependency.

Tactical Fit Score (TFS, 0–100)

Directly measures alignment between a candidate's tactical profile and Rajasthan Royals' specific squad vacancies (death bowling, lower-order finisher, middle-overs spin).

TFS = 100 * Role_Gap_Criticality_Weight

Calibrated against audited RR gap severities: Death Specialist (90.0), Finisher (87.5), Middle Spinner (80.0).

Acquisition Risk Score (ARS, 0–100)

Composite recruitment risk combining auction bidding inflation probability (star-power demand) and overseas roster friction penalty (+6.0 overseas surcharge).

ARS = Base_Bidding_Risk + (6.0 if Overseas else 0.0)

* Lower is better. ARS enters negatively into the RDH calculation to reward disciplined, cost-effective recruitment.

Recruitment Decision Heuristic (RDH, 0–90 Scale)

Synthesizes performance, reliability, stability, and tactical fit while penalizing acquisition risk. Operates on a strict 0.00 to 90.00 theoretical scale.

RDH = clip(0.30*PR + 0.20*ERS + 0.20*SSS + 0.20*TFS - 0.10*ARS, 0.0, 90.0)

* Theoretical maximum is 90.00 because positive weights sum to 0.90. Never displayed as a percentage or win probability.
